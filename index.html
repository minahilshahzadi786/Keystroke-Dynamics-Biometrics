🎓 Mentor Presentation & Defense Guide

Q1: What are the two core features of Keystroke Dynamics?
1. Dwell Time ($D_k$): The duration a key is held down ($release(k) - press(k)$).
2. Flight Time ($F_{k1,k2}$): The latency between releasing key 1 and pressing key 2 ($press(k2) - release(k1)$). Can be negative during fast rollover typing!
Q2: Why use Variance-Weighted Distance instead of simple Euclidean distance?
Simple Euclidean distance treats all key gaps equally. But some gaps vary naturally (e.g. shift key switches), while others are extremely consistent. Weighting by standard deviation ($\sigma_i$) penalizes deviations on your most stable keys much more heavily.
Q3: How do you prevent division by zero when standard deviation is zero?
We apply $\epsilon$-smoothing ($\epsilon = 10\text{ms}$). The variance-weighted formula becomes: |attempt_i - μ_i| / (σ_i + ε). This ensures numerical stability.
Q4: What real-world standards govern behavioral biometrics?
Behavioral biometrics are governed by ISO/IEC 19792 (Biometric Performance Testing) and NIST risk-based continuous authentication guidelines.
